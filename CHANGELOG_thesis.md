## 3. Editorial & Technical Corrections Across Chapters

- **Nomenclature Uniformity:** All references to "FDA Pure", "DualTrack-Pure", "NODISC", "BSTART", and "NOGUARD" completely eliminated. Uniform name **FDA-RoLoRA** applied throughout.
- **LoRA Matrix Convention & Dimensions (Chapter 1, Eq. 1.1):**
  - Standard column-vector convention established: $W_0 \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{out}} \times d_{\text{in}}}$, down-projection $A \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times d_{\text{in}}}$, up-projection $B \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{out}} \times r}$, $\Delta W = \frac{\alpha}{r} B A$.
  - FFA-LoRA aggregation equation corrected to $\frac{1}{N}\sum B_i A = (\frac{1}{N}\sum B_i) A$.
- **RoLoRA Row-Vector Notation Clarification (Chapter 2, Figure 2.4 caption):**
  - Added explicit note clarifying that Chen et al. schematic uses row-vector ($AB$) notation while thesis follows standard column-vector ($BA$) notation.
- **Rank Exclusivity ($r=4, \alpha=8$):**
  - Stray references to $r=8, 16$ as experimental settings removed from Chapters 2, 3, 4, and 6. Strictly stated $r=4, \alpha_{\text{lora}}=8$.
- **Cold Start & Alternating Optimization (Chapter 3):**
  - Cold start described exactly as implemented: $\theta_A = 0, \theta_B = 0, e_{\min}=1$, no first-round guard (initial single-epoch pass on B and A bootstraps dynamic thresholds).
  - B-first alternating optimization justified ($B=0$ at initialization, so $B$ trains first while freezing $A$).
  - Corrected monitoring traffic explanation (evaluating subspace variance requires communicating local drifts or 6 KB AMS sketches, adding 2.5%–5.8% overhead).
  - Corrected SVD computational complexity claim from $\mathcal{O}(d^3)$ to $\mathcal{O}(d^2 r)$ and highlighted dense matrix communication blowup ($\mathcal{O}(d^2)$).
- **Refutation of Regime-Reversal Myth (Chapter 5):**
  - Removed claim that non-IID skew causes FDA to "brake to $\bar{e} \approx 2.5\text{--}3.8$". Replaced with data-grounded analysis showing realized non-IID epochs reach $8.40\text{--}17.76$ epochs.
  - Refuted "Matrix B deeper training" claim with empirical symmetry metrics ($\bar{e}_A = 10.63 \pm 0.45, \bar{e}_B = 10.82 \pm 0.41$).
  - Corrected QNLI $C=50$ Non-IID early-stopping framing (divergence under aggressive $k=1.0$ vs. stability under conservative $k=0.5$).
  - Corrected SST-2 $C=50$ RoLoRA behavior (failed to escape initial majority-class plateau within budget, not collapsed from trained state).
- **Bibliography Corrections (`references.bib`):**
  - Added original FDA EDBT 2025 paper (Theologitis et al., EDBT 2025).
  - Added FDA-Opt arXiv DOI and preprint details.
  - Removed spurious "LoRA-the-Explorer" entry.
